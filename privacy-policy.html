Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Client-Side Tool Processing

All developer utilities on LaraTools.dev (including SQL analyzers, DTO generators, and HMAC signature debuggers) run locally in your web browser via JavaScript. Inputs, secret keys, JSON payloads, and SQL queries entered into these tools are never transmitted to, stored on, or logged by our servers.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements and keep these developer utilities free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visit to LaraTools.dev and/or other sites on the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

3. Analytics & Log Files

Standard hosting access logs (such as anonymized IP addresses, browser user-agent, referring pages, and timestamps) may be collected by our hosting provider for security, DDoS mitigation, and aggregate traffic measurement.
